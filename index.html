01 · About

Research with an engineering mindset and a teaching mission.

My work combines sensing, machine learning, and computing to extract meaningful information from real-world human behavior. I am particularly interested in wearable systems, multimodal learning, physical activity and energy expenditure estimation, egocentric vision, and deployable AI for health applications.

Alongside research, I value teaching, mentoring, and the translation of complex technical ideas into structured, practical learning experiences. My academic background spans electrical and computer engineering, intelligent networks, image and signal processing, and embedded systems.
